### Fair-Code: The Freedom to Innovate Without Limits

For years, the market has been dominated by closed SaaS solutions (like Zapier or Make) which, although powerful, impose rigid limits: costs that explode with volumes, data transiting on third-party servers, and the inability to customize the code. n8n changes this paradigm with its **Fair-code** approach.

Being distributed with accessible source code, n8n allows hosting directly on company servers (on-premise). This offers crucial advantages for those who are serious:
*   **Data Security**: Sensitive information never leaves the corporate perimeter, a fundamental requirement for [cybersecurity](/en/solutions/cybersecurity) and GDPR compliance.
*   **No Vendor Lock-in**: You have complete control over the platform.
*   **Scalability**: Run millions of workflows without worrying about the cost per single operation.

This philosophy perfectly matches the approach oriented towards [open source software](/en/solutions/open-source-software) that we recommend to guarantee independence and longevity to IT solutions.

### AI Native: When Automation Becomes Intelligent

The real leap in quality of n8n lies in its deep integration with Artificial Intelligence. While other tools are trying to add AI as an accessory \"feature\", n8n has integrated it into the heart of the system.

Thanks to native nodes for LangChain and compatibility with major LLM models (such as GPT-4, Claude, or local models), it is possible to create **AI Agents** that do not just answer questions, but **act**. Imagine a workflow that:
1.  Receives a support request email.
2.  Uses AI to analyze the tone and classify urgency.
3.  Queries the CRM or management system via robust [systems integration](/en/solutions/systems-integration) to retrieve the customer's history.
4.  Generates a personalized draft response or opens a technical ticket, notifying the correct team.

This level of automation transforms raw data into decisions, enhancing corporate [Business Intelligence](/en/solutions/business-intelligence).
