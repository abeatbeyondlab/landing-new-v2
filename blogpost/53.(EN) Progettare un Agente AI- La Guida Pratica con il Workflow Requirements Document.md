### What is an AI Agent and Why Planning is Everything

An AI agent is an autonomous software system that perceives its environment (through data and inputs) and acts to achieve specific goals (such as responding to a customer or optimizing a logistics process). It can range from a simple chatbot to a complex demand forecasting system.

Without a plan, you are just guessing. The WRD forces you to answer the hard questions *before* they become expensive problems, ensuring that the technological investment is aligned with business goals, a fundamental principle of our [software strategy consulting](/en/solutions/software-strategy-consulting-and-advisory).

### A Practical Example: AI Agent for Lead Qualification

Let's imagine we want to **design an AI agent** that qualifies incoming leads from the website.

*   **Goal:** Reply to qualified leads within 5 minutes, 24/7.
*   **Triggers & Data:** Trigger: new lead sent from the site form. Data to analyze: email, company role, message text, navigation data (if available).
*   **Decision Logic:** The agent analyzes the message text with Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify urgency and interest. Checks the email domain to see if it is a company. Cross-references data with an industry database to estimate company size.
*   **Actions & Output:** If the lead is qualified (e.g., manager of a company > 50 employees with a specific request), the agent sends a personalized email and creates an urgent task in the CRM for a salesperson. If not qualified, sends a standard resource.
*   **Error Handling:** If the analysis fails, the lead is marked as \"for manual review\" and notified to an operator.

This level of detail, defined in the WRD, is what distinguishes a successful project from a failed experiment.
