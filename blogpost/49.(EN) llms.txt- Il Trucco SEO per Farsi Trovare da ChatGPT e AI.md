### What Exactly Is an llms.txt File and Why Does It Concern You?

Think of the **llms.txt** file as a conceptual map of your website, created specifically for Large Language Models (LLMs). Just as `robots.txt` gives instructions to crawlers and `sitemap.xml` lists your pages, **llms.txt** provides AIs with a structured summary of your most important content. It is a simple text file in Markdown format, placed in the main folder of your site (`yoursite.com/llms.txt`).

Its purpose is brilliant in its simplicity: helping an AI understand what your site is about, what the key sections are, and where to find relevant information, all without having to parse the entire HTML code, menus, and scripts of every page. This is not a simple technical nicety, but a decision that falls within a broader [software strategy consulting](/en/solutions/software-strategy-consulting-and-advisory) to prepare your business for the future.

### Traditional SEO vs. AI SEO (AIO): The New Battlefield

The fundamental difference lies in the goal. Classic SEO aims to position a page at the top of a list of results. AIO, on the other hand, aims to ensure that the AI *understands* and *uses* your content correctly to formulate a complex and coherent answer. Models like ChatGPT have a limited \"context window\": they cannot \"read\" an entire site at once. The **llms.txt** file solves this problem by offering a clean and targeted summary.

Here is a comparison to clarify ideas:

| Feature | Traditional SEO (for Google) | AIO - AI Optimization (for ChatGPT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Goal** | Ranking in SERP (list of links) | Being the authoritative source in the AI's response |
| **Focus** | Keywords, backlinks, speed | Context, logical structure, content synthesis |
| **Key Tool** | `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, Schema.org | `llms.txt`, clear and structured content |
| **Output** | Visibility of a specific page | Inclusion of your know-how in the generated response |

Adopting an AIO strategy means shaping the information architecture so that it is digestible for machines, a task that requires forward-looking [IT architecture design](/en/solutions/it-architecture-design).

### The Perfect Structure: How to Create an Effective llms.txt File

Creating an **llms.txt** is simple. Use Markdown syntax to define a clear hierarchy. A well-structured file should include:

1.  **Main Title (H1)**: The name of your site or company.
2.  **Short Description (Blockquote)**: A sentence summarizing your mission.
3.  **Thematic Sections (H2)**: The macro-areas of your site (e.g., `## Services`, `## Resources`, `## About Us`).
4.  **Links with Description (List)**: Under each section, list links to the most important pages, each with a brief description of its content.
5.  **Optional Section (H2)**: An area for secondary content that the AI can ignore if it has little mental \"space\".

Here is a practical example for an IT consulting company:

```markdown
# A Beat Beyond
> We provide tailored IT services to help SMEs increase efficiency.

This file provides a map of our services and core competencies.

## Strategy and IT Consulting
- [Software Strategy Consulting](/en/solutions/software-strategy-consulting-and-advisory): We define IT strategies to align technology with your business goals.
- [IT Architecture Design](/en/solutions/it-architecture-design): We design robust, scalable, and secure IT infrastructures.

## Operational Solutions
- [Systems Integration (ERP, MES)](/en/solutions/systems-integration): We optimize data flows between your software systems.
```

### Operational Checklist to Implement llms.txt

Follow these steps to put this AI SEO trick into practice immediately:

- [ ] **Create the File**: Open a text editor and write the content following the Markdown structure.
- [ ] **Be Selective**: Do not list every page. Choose only the resources that define who you are and what you do. Synthesis is key.
- [ ] **Upload to Root**: Save the file as `llms.txt` and upload it to the main directory of your site.
- [ ] **Check Links**: Ensure all links work correctly.
- [ ] **Keep Updated**: This file is a living document. Update it whenever you add important services or content. ITS maintenance must be part of proper [IT project management](/en/solutions/project-management).
